# Owning Your AI: Hardware and Training for Private Local Models

**Christopher Maglio and Muse** — 23 October 2026

[Read the paper (PDF)](Owning_Your_AI_Oct_26.pdf)

Why private AI needs so much memory, what hardware it takes, and when to train: a handy-sized guide from the math to the purchase decision, with Glimmer and Spark Open as worked examples.

Contents: the memory wall for local models; hardware options at each scale; retrieval (RAG) versus LoRA versus full fine-tuning, and when each is the right spend; the catastrophic-forgetting tax on training; and a purchase decision you can defend.
